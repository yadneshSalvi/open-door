Try the furniture move before anyone has to lift it.

Open Door helps people planning space around a bed explore a wheelchair turn and check the door separately. Move the table, undo a change, compare three proposed layouts, and inspect the chosen corner at real size. A QR link opens the same room in the companion for a fresh check.

A SPECS Lens demonstrated with an entered room model in Lens Studio Preview.

Try the companion: https://open-door-weld.vercel.app
