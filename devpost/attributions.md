Editorial serif headings use a system fallback; no Georgia file is redistributed.

The installed Snap SIK/UI Kit packages, official 3D Hints v2 and its compatible dependencies come from the starter/Asset Library and remain subject to Snap's terms and notices. Open Door does not claim their authorship. Native cube and base-material inputs are supplied through the actual Lens project with their existing provenance. Rounded furniture meshes are authored procedural geometry. The images in `media/` are actual application/Preview captures; any outer-margin crops are disclosed there.

The spatial revision adds original algorithmic contact, rolling and completion WAV cues, generated with the installed CLAD `build-sfx` engine (Apache-2.0). They contain no sampled recording or licensed song. The [generator](../scripts/generate-native-audio.cjs) and [sample counts/provenance](../lens-src/Audio/provenance.json) are retained. UIKit's interaction feedback and the official Leaf package remain separately attributed to Snap; the LEAF scenario code is authored for this project. The wheelchair silhouette is a schematic decoration constrained to the supplied profile, not a scanned or fitted device mesh.

Configuration-space search, separating axes, distance bounds and A* are established techniques. This project's contribution is their explicit task/revision contract, conservative checks, inspectable evidence and spatial room-editing workflow. It does not claim to invent these algorithms or be the first accessibility-related AR tool.

References include [Snap Internet Access](https://developers.snap.com/spectacles/about-spectacles-features/apis/internet-access), [Next.js Route Handlers](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/getting-started/route-handlers), [OpenAI Structured Outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs), installed package source and Lens type declarations.

The [Devpost Learn plan-lens framework](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs) informed the original design brief and intended room task. Later review rounds drove the surface staging, pinchable chair and indoor corner. CLAD and Lens Studio MCP were development tools. These acknowledgments do not describe a participant interview or user-study result.
