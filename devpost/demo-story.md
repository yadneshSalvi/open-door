# Proposed demonstration

Target: 2 minutes 40 seconds of the **actual SPECS Lens**, based on the [accepted native repair flow](native-repair-preview.md). This remains a capture plan; no finished recording or publication is implied.

The story is a spatial choice: two blocked tasks, computed furniture changes, visible movement versus clearance, one accepted plan, and the full occupied footprint rehearsing its route. The synthetic room/profile stays identified. The locked bed, dimensions and task do not change to manufacture a pass.

The four official judging criteria have equal weight, with Design first for ties. This sequence supports Design through spatial clarity and recovery, Technical Implementation through verified geometry and source identity, Innovation/Idea through a computed movement/clearance choice, and Presentation through a complete working journey.
