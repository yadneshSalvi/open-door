# Open Door

**Make room for your everyday.**

A pleasing arrangement can still fail a specific everyday task. Open Door helps someone inspect an explicit occupied footprint, rehearse a bedside journey, understand a separate door conflict and choose changes while preserving what matters.

The SPECS Lens presents a miniature architectural model with contextual controls and intended-scale inspection. Its TypeScript engine checks the full oriented footprint, intermediate movement and final heading. A companion offers dimension editing, live evidence, reviewed language preferences and SVG/JSON plans. A QR carries the exact native revision into that companion.

The provided-room example has two independent constraints: moving the table clears the route, but the bench must also move before the door sweep clears. Results are computed, not scripted success states. Arbitrary edits produce fresh results.

This prototype uses explicit synthetic geometry. It does not scan a real room or certify accessibility. Transfers, force, comfort and physical hardware remain outside the demonstrated evidence. No testimonial or external-study outcome is claimed.
